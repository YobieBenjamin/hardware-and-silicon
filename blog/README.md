The two posts that lead into 01 are in the [Watermark](https://github.com/YobieBenjamin/Watermark/tree/main/blog) repository: [Why an Invisible Stamp Won't Keep AI Safe](https://github.com/YobieBenjamin/Watermark/blob/main/blog/01-why-an-invisible-stamp-wont-keep-ai-safe.md) (for everyone) and [Inside OpenAI's Watermark: What the Study Found](https://github.com/YobieBenjamin/Watermark/blob/main/blog/02-inside-openais-watermark.md) (the deep technical companion). Post 04 is the comprehensive silicon design that 01 promised; it was revised after a chip designer's review ([`docs/REVIEW-2026-10.md`](../docs/REVIEW-2026-10.md)).

Figures for the series are in [`figures/`](figures/) and are rendered by [`tools/make_figures.py`](tools/make_figures.py). The Word files for 02–04 are built from the Markdown by [`tools/build.js`](tools/build.js) (`make blog-series`; needs Node and the `docx` package); 01 is built with `pandoc` (`make blog`). Every link in the posts is checked by [`tools/check_links.py`](tools/check_links.py) (`make blog-links`): it reports each URL as ok, dead, or behind a bot wall (sites such as publishers, LinkedIn and .gov that answer scripts differently from browsers), and fails only on dead links.
